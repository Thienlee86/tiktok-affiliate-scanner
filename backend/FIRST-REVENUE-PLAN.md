# First confirmed revenue plan

Objective: obtain the first attributable confirmed commission with minimal manual work and zero paid acquisition.

1. Scanner produces eligible candidates from confirmed API metrics.
2. Momentum history adds a small priority boost when a product is rising.
3. Revenue Engine marks products with confirmed orders/revenue.
4. Experiment Queue prioritizes high-score, rising products that have not yet produced confirmed revenue.
5. One small organic affiliate experiment is run at a time so attribution remains interpretable.
6. After confirmed revenue, feed product-level conversion and net revenue back into prioritization.

Do not spend on ads for this phase. Do not treat clicks, pending orders, estimated commission, or self-purchases as proof of revenue. The success event is platform-confirmed attributable commission.

Human-only checkpoints: account login/consent, platform-required publishing actions, and any action involving secrets or identity verification.
